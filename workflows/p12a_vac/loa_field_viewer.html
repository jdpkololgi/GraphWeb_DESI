Independent atlas tiles are not a coherent whole-survey posterior. Real-DESI calibration is unverified; survey boundaries and the simulation prior affect these reconstructions.

How to read this view · provenance and limits

Drag to rotate; scroll to zoom; use the toolbar to save a PNG. Positions are actual equatorial Cartesian comoving coordinates, in Mpc/h. Axis scales are equal; neither cap is rotated to look more web-like. Keyboard users can operate every view, sample and visibility control; the dense 3D marks are not individually keyboard navigable.

The detailed boxes show 16 locally coherent density draws and a deterministically thinned sample of observed context galaxies inside each box. Density surfaces use display-only 2× pooling and masked marching-cubes meshes at the selected density level. The atlas shows voxels above that level, from 8 independently generated draws per tile, with no blending across seams. Hover gives the tile index. A sample number across separate tiles is a visual mosaic, never a joint cosmic realization. The mean averages density before taking log₁₀.

Galaxies trace a flux-limited, incomplete survey. Empty or masked areas are not evidence of a physical void. Only display voxels with support ≥ 0.5 are shown. Thresholding density for display is not the tidal-tensor cosmic-web classification used in the property analysis. Color is log10 density, fixed from 0 to 1.5; values above the scale remain in the data.

Generated locally from the frozen September 30, 2026 Loa atlas. No external scripts, analytics, or data requests. The companion VIEWER.json records source hashes and displayed counts. Rendering requires WebGL; on slow machines use a detailed box or galaxy-only overview.
