# Posterior transition audit

See the [simulation-side report and figures](../../TNG/Illustris/docs/p12a_transition_audit_20260929.md).
The saved exposed ph00650k evaluation was tested at all three class boundaries,
using its512 saved posterior draws. No VAC value, selection or model was changed.

Posterior-selected transition candidates retain approximately nominal interval
coverage and93–96% mean probability in the relevant adjacent classes. However,
only62% of true near-knot galaxies are flagged ambiguous, versus72% and70% for
the other boundaries;22% of true knots just above the threshold receive less
than or equal to10% knot probability. Void–wall crossing probabilities also show
a5–6 percentage-point reliability residual in some well-populated bins.

Thus the posterior carries useful ambiguity beyond hard labels, but cannot be
claimed to recover every transition or a spatially coherent boundary surface.
ph006 was used for model selection, and no real-DESI truth was available.
Existing provisional-release and population-mismatch limitations remain.
